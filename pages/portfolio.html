Murshid — B2B Clinic Management & Offline Dietetics CRM

A comprehensive administrative and organizational platform designed specifically for dietitians and the management of health centers and clinics. Built to save time, automate administrative reports, and calculate vital and nutritional indicators for patients, while providing maximum privacy protection via fully encrypted offline local storage.

Administrative and Operational Features
✓ An integrated CRM system to manage patient records and organize appointments and visits.
✓ A smart assistant that reviews nutritional schedules and detects ingredient conflicts to alert the specialist.
✓ Instant analysis and extraction of laboratory test data from images (Stateless OCR).
✓ Offline localized calculators to compute BMI, nutritional needs, and pediatric growth indicators.
✓ Export premium structured reports in PDF format, printable and customizable with your clinic's logo.
✓ 100% internet-independent operational architecture to save health records locally on the device.

02 · Bariatric Surgery Post-Op Companion

Bariatric Surgery Companion

Bariatric Post-Op Tracker — Recovery Checklist & Diet Phase Ecosystem

A digital and organizational companion designed to support and track the self-recovery phase of bariatric surgery patients (like sleeve gastrectomy and gastric bypass) post-operation. The app aims to empower patients to manage the progression of dietary phases and hydration behaviors, schedule periodic appointments, and log daily intake to ensure a safe, sustainable recovery that complies with global guidelines (ASMBS).

Behavioral and Organizational Features
✓ Tracking the six gradual post-op dietary phases (from clear liquids up to regular foods).
✓ A smart daily journal to monitor fluid and protein intake and record goal achievements.
✓ An intelligent reminder system that separates calcium and iron supplement doses to prevent absorption conflicts.
✓ Scheduling periodic appointments and required post-op tests, reminding the patient to adhere to surgeon visits.
✓ Behavioral educational guidelines to overcome side effects such as Dumping Syndrome.
✓ Fully secure local storage on the patient's phone to protect the privacy of their records and daily activity.
💙 Flutter ✨ Local Databases 📅 Recovery Schedules 🔒 Client-Side Encryption 🥤 Hydration Logs
🏥 Designed for full integration and compliance with the guidelines of the American Society for Metabolic and Bariatric Surgery (ASMBS).
