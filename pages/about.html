About Us

From Academic Vision to Technical Sovereignty and Data Security

FES HealthTech was founded in response to a deep gap in the healthcare software sector: the lack of ultra-advanced digital tools for healthcare practitioners and specialists that match the efficiency of major global systems, while simultaneously respecting the absolute sovereignty of patient data and adhering to strict security and privacy protocols.

Our name FES carries two integrated dimensions: it stands as an acronym for our three operational pillars: Functional Excellence & Security, and simultaneously references the well-known biophysical medical term (Functional Electrical Stimulation) as a symbol of the technical activation and stimulation we bring to healthcare software infrastructure.

Our research and development at FES HealthTech follows a rigorous approach that bridges academic theory with secure engineering application. Our technical solutions are based on building specialized software kernels and intelligent query engines to streamline clinic management, daily consultations, and nutritional planning efficiently, while strictly preserving the highest standards of local health data privacy.

Alliances & Technical Integration

Interoperability and Software Compatibility

Our systems rely on unified operational standards and hybrid (cloud and local) infrastructure that ensure security and integration with major healthcare institutions.

Google Cloud Infrastructure

We utilize secure Google Cloud services for Firestore data management, authentication, and AI, with isolated identities.

Decentralized Security Hardening

A localized encryption architecture using AES-256 natively on the practitioner's and patient's devices, without sharing keys outside the device.

HL7 & FHIR Integration

Technical compatibility and standardized API connections with global HL7 and FHIR specifications to facilitate interoperability with medical systems.

Stateless OCR Processing

Extracting data and laboratory tests instantly from images without storing any copies on servers.
